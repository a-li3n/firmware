I am using a UEDX80480050E-WB-A display panel: ESP32-S3, 16MB Flash, 8MB PSRAM. The panel is parallel RGB. I am an analog electrical engineer by trade and I am rather new to coding - especially embedded designs.

I have a requirement to create thermal images with an MLX90640, send them via ESP-NOW from one ESP32 to another. The second (Intermediary) talks to my display panel with SPI. I have fully tested the ESP-NOW link (SOLID) and the SPI communication (SOLID). All my signals are high quality as is my power rails.

Problem: my display panel is configured as a GUI with screens for multiple functions. One screen is the Thermal Camera Screen. When I enter the thermal camera screen, one thermal image is displayed but then freezes - the crazy part is that when I touch the screen, images flow, if I remove my finger, they stop. This is not a noise related issue.

I have to be careful about what code files to share. I am using VS Code/PlatformIO/Arduino Framework. LVGL 8.3.4 and LovyanGFX 1.2.7. Could you tell me what files I would need to send for you to be able to help with this issue? I am trying to narrow down the exact place that is having issues and I feel confident that the interaction of GFX/LVGL is the most likely place but I cannot figure this out.

[spi\_link.cpp](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/22938579/spi_link.cpp)

[lvgl\_port.cpp](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/22938582/lvgl_port.cpp)
